# Experimental

Use for a deliberate moment that changes how the reader perceives or handles the
product — where the interesting claim is not what the page says but how it
behaves.

## What makes it work rather than merely strange

**It is about the subject.** Novelty grounded in what the product actually is
survives contact with a reader; novelty grounded in a technique does not.
"Because this is what this thing is like" is the whole justification, and if you
cannot finish that sentence you are decorating.

**The reader can find the edges.** They should be able to work out what they are
doing, undo it, and get to the ordinary product from inside it. Legibility is
not a compromise imposed on the experiment — an experiment nobody can operate
produced no experience to evaluate.

**It sits inside conventional structure.** The surrounding page being disciplined
and unsurprising is what lets one moment be surprising. Two unrelated
experiments cancel: each becomes the noise the other is heard against.

## Prototyping

Build the riskiest part first and in isolation, before the page depends on it.
The point is to find out early whether the idea is any good, which is worth
knowing while you can still change your mind. Most experimental ideas do not
survive being built, and that is the process working.

If none of the bounded systems in `../systems/NATIVE_FOUNDATIONS.md` fits,
build a focused project-native prototype. Do not go shopping through a larger
effect list for something that will fit — that reverses the order and produces a
technique in search of a subject.

## How this fails

Cursor toys. Distortion applied to nothing in particular. Random physics.
Navigation hidden to make it interesting. A mechanism whose removal makes the
product clearer at no loss of meaning, which is the plainest evidence it was
never about the subject.

And the failure the reject-list above tends to cause on its own: a page with no
experiment in it, which trips none of these and was chosen precisely because it
trips none of these. Nothing here asks you to be safe. It asks the risk to be
about the product.
