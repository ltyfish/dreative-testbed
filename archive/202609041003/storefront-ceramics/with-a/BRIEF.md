# Brief — Kilnwork — a small ceramics shop (with-a Dreative)

This is the shop for Kilnwork, a two-person ceramics studio that makes nine pieces and sells them directly. Buyers are choosing between this and a supermarket mug at a tenth of the price, so the page has to make the difference visible rather than asserted. Design and build that shop.

`src/App.jsx` holds the required content and behaviour as unstyled markup in no meaningful order, and `src/styles.css` is empty. There is no existing design to keep or improve. Decide what sections this page has, in what order, and what it looks like.

Use the Dreative skill for this work. It is installed in this project. Build the recommended direction; treat this message as the user's explicit choice of direction and settings. Nobody is available to answer during this session, so take the recommended configuration and build — do not pause for any confirmation.

Work only inside this directory. Preserve the following, which are product requirements rather than design opinions:
- All nine products with their exact names, prices, dimensions, capacity where stated, and stock state, including the two that are sold out and cannot be added to the bag
- The three glazes with their names and the note that each fires differently and no two pieces match exactly
- The per-product glaze availability — not every piece is offered in every glaze
- The bag, which must name each line's product, glaze, quantity, and price, and show a running total
- The care facts: dishwasher and microwave safety per piece, and that the unglazed foot will mark a soft surface
- The making statement: thrown and fired in the studio, the firing schedule, and the six-week lead time on restocks
- Both customer notes with their names and what they bought
- The shipping and returns statement — free over 60, 30 days, and that breakage in transit is replaced

When you are done, make sure `npm run build` succeeds. Do not commit anything.
