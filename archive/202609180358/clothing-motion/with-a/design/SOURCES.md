# Cloth source material

Every photograph below is **material/fabric photography used as representative cloth**. None is
a photograph of Marlow & Vale inventory — Marlow & Vale is fictional. Each plate in
`design/_src/cloth/` is a crop of the listed source, converted to a duotone in the garment's
stated colour so the nine plates read as one art-directed set.

## Attribution obligations carried into the build

- CC BY-SA sources (`overshirt`, `trouser` — Donegal Tweed by Toxophilus; `shorts` — Cordjacke detail by Anna reg, Wikimedia Commons) require credit and share-alike on the derivative plate.
- CC BY source (`chore` — Blue Denim Fabric Texture by Pink Sherbet Photography, Wikimedia Commons) requires credit.
- CC0 sources (`oxford`, `tee`, `jean`, `knit`, `cardigan` — rawpixel / StockSnap) require none, but are credited anyway.
- The shipped site will carry these credits in the footer alongside the representative-imagery disclosure.

## Rejected

- A Harris Tweed photograph (Wikimedia, CC BY-SA) was initially used for the Pleated Trouser plate and **removed**: it shows the Harris Tweed certification orb, a third-party trade mark that must not appear on a fictional label's cloth.
