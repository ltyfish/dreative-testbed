# Photography credits

Every photograph on this page is a real, licensed macro or documentary photograph of a
mechanical watch movement or of watchmaking. They are reference photographs of the
craft, not photographs of Caliber 08. Crops and colour grading are ours; where the
source licence is share-alike, the derivative is offered under the same licence.
