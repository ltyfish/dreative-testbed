Returns

If something is wrong with a bag, write to us.

We have not published a boilerplate returns policy, because at our size every case is answered by one of the two people who roasted the coffee rather than by a form.

Tell us the order, the coffee, and what went wrong. The contact form reaches the same inbox that sits next to the machine, and we reply within a day.

Before you write

Two things worth checking first, because they explain most surprises: the roast date printed on the bag, and the roast level. A coffee dropped just after first crack and one taken to the edge of second crack are the same bean and taste nothing alike — the roast ladder shows exactly where each of ours stops.
