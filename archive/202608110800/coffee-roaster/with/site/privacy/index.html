Privacy

This site does almost nothing with your data.

The list below describes what this website actually does when you load it, not what a template says a website usually does.

None
No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising identifiers.
None
No cookies are set and nothing is written to local storage.
Zero
Third-party requests. The typefaces, photographs and scripts are all served from this domain, so no other company sees that you visited.

The contact form

What you type into the email and message fields stays in your browser until you submit it. When you do, it goes to the roastery inbox and nowhere else. We keep it as long as it takes to answer you, and we do not add you to a mailing list from it.

The cart

Items you add are held in the page for the length of your visit and are gone when you close the tab.

Asking us about it

If you want to know what we hold about you, or want it deleted, ask via the contact form. The same two people answer that as answer everything else.
