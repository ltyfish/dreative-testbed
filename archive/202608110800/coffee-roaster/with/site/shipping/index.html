Shipping

Everything leaves Bergen the day it is roasted.

We roast to order in 12kg batches and pack straight off the cooling tray. There is no warehouse and no standing stock, which is the whole reason the timings below are short.

Rates and delivery times

Rates outside our standing subscription depend on where you are and what you are ordering, so we quote them per order rather than publishing a table we would have to keep wrong. Send us a message with your country and roughly what you want, and you will have an answer within a day.

Wholesale

Cafés and offices are handled the same way, on the same machine, in the same 12kg batches. Use the same contact form — it reaches the two people who do the roasting.
