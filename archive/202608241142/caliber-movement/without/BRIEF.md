This is the product page for Caliber 08, a mechanical watch movement made by Aubry & Vent in a run of 200. It is bought by collectors who care about how a movement is built and how it runs, and who will spend real time on this page before reserving one. Design and build that page.

`src/App.jsx` holds the required content and behaviour as unstyled markup in no meaningful order, and `src/styles.css` is empty. There is no existing design to keep or improve. Decide what sections this page has, in what order, and what it looks like.

Do not use any design skill, framework, or checklist beyond your own judgement.

Work only inside this directory. Preserve the following, which are product requirements rather than design opinions:
- The six stages of the power path, in the order energy travels through them, each with its detail line and its figure
- The four physical layers with their thicknesses and notes
- Every row of the specification list
- All three finishes with their exact CHF prices, delivery dates, and remaining allocation
- The five workshop facts, including the run of 200 and the indefinite servicing commitment
- The reservation form and its confirmation, which must name the chosen finish, its price, and its delivery date
- The statement that reservations are not binding and no payment is taken

When you are done, make sure `npm run build` succeeds. Do not commit anything.
