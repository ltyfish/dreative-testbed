# Brief — Marlow & Vale — an ordinary clothing shop (with-a Dreative)

This is the shop for Marlow & Vale, a small clothing label with eleven staff, one shop in Leeds, and nine garments for sale. There is no story here beyond clothes that are made well and priced the same all year — the buyer is choosing between this and any other clothing site open in another tab, and has to be able to see the clothes, find their size, and buy. Design and build that shop.

`src/App.jsx` holds the required content and behaviour as unstyled markup in no meaningful order, and `src/styles.css` is empty. There is no existing design to keep or improve. Decide what sections this page has, in what order, and what it looks like.

Use the Dreative skill for this work. It is installed in this project. Build the recommended direction; treat this message as the user's explicit choice of direction and settings. Nobody is available to answer during this session, so take the recommended configuration and build — do not pause for any confirmation.

Work only inside this directory. Preserve the following, which are product requirements rather than design opinions:
- All nine garments with their exact names, categories, prices, colour lists, fabric statements, and detail sentences
- The per-garment sold-out sizes, which cannot be added to the bag: XS on the Oxford Shirt, S and XL on the Chore Jacket, M on the Pleated Trouser, L on the Lambswool Crew, XS and S on the Shawl Cardigan, XL on the Camp Short
- The category filter, including its counts and the All state
- Choosing a size before adding to the bag, and the bag itself, which must name each line's garment, size, and price, allow a line to be removed, and show a running subtotal
- The delivery thresholds and the amount still needed to reach free delivery over £100
- The full size chart — every size row with its chest, waist, and sleeve measurement in centimetres — and the between-sizes advice
- Every care fact, including which garments cannot be machine washed, that nothing goes in a tumble dryer, that the denim is unwashed and will bleed, and the repair offer
- The shipping and returns statement for all three regions, the 60 day return window, and the same-day exchange
- All three customer reviews with their names and what they bought
- The three shop facts: eleven people and one shop in Leeds, four named factories, and that there are never any sales

When you are done, make sure `npm run build` succeeds. Do not commit anything.
