# Independent visual critic — Northwind Roast Ledger

Reviewed 2026-07-18 from the closed evidence manifest only. I inspected the six declared final screenshots at 1440px, 390px and 320px. The two WebM files were present, but no supported video decoder was available in this critic context, so I did not infer motion quality from filenames or builder claims.

## Verdict

**MAJOR REVISION REQUIRED**

The route is visually authored, cohesive and unusually disciplined for a commerce landing page. Its editorial typography, near-black/parchment alternation, orange roast accent, documentary coffee imagery and ledger-like controls create a recognizable Northwind character. The origin selector and brew vessel are real content-specific peaks, and the mobile route is substantially recomposed rather than simply shrunk.

However, the supplied visible states do not yet demonstrate the approved concept at Experimental / all-treatment strength. After the hero, the “persistent industrial chronometer and chapter bus” is no longer perceptibly continuous; the route reads primarily as a sequence of refined editorial panels. The origin ring and brew vessel are strong isolated instruments, but the evidence does not show one living apparatus changing semantic role across chapters. Spatial/3D contribution is also not visually legible beyond layered graphic composition. Motion, physical interaction response, cross-section handoffs and reduced-motion translation remain unjudgeable from the six permitted stills.

## Treatment review

- **UX — pass with caveat:** product, cart, subscription and contact affordances are visible and semantically direct, but screenshots cannot prove outcomes.
- **Mobile — pass:** 390px and 320px are recomposed with stacked selector, dossier, brew steps and commerce controls. Type becomes very small at 320px, and the expanded menu interrupts rather than integrates with the ledger composition.
- **Refined — pass:** typography, palette, rules, spacing and restrained controls are consistently art-directed.
- **Motion — insufficient evidence:** still-state differences imply change, but no directly viewable temporal evidence was available.
- **Interaction — partial:** the origin calibration and brew phase controls are content-specific, but input response and physicality are not established by stills.
- **Media — pass:** documentary origin and roaster imagery is integrated into the composition; the nine-slice reveal is visible rather than described only.
- **3D — major shortfall:** the evidence reads as layered 2D/diagrammatic composition. No materially spatial object, viewpoint change, depth behavior or fallback comparison is perceptible.
- **Immersive — partial:** the shared palette and ledger language provide continuity, but the approved persistent instrument does not visibly survive the route.
- **Cinematic — partial:** opening framing and alternating rests are strong, yet structural handoffs and temporal pacing cannot be confirmed.
- **Experimental — partial:** origin calibration and brew transmutation are distinctive peaks, but the full route remains more editorial-refined than experimentally transformative.

## Peaks and mechanisms

The **origin-calibration** peak is the strongest evidence-backed moment: selected origin, profile number, dossier, price and add target form one purposeful instrument. The **brew-transmutation** peak is visually specific and memorable, though only its phase-1 and phase-4 resolved states are visible; temporal causality is not.

Of the five mechanisms, **ledger-media-reveal** is visibly successful, **origin-calibration** is visually convincing, and **brew-transmutation** is partially established. **roast-stage** and **batch-journey** do not meet their perceptual burden in the supplied stills: the chronometer does not visibly persist through downstream chapters, and cross-section transformation cannot be evaluated.

## Hero-removed test

The route still has authored product calibration, an editorial review rest, a bespoke brew instrument, subscription resolution and contact utility after removing the hero. It therefore passes the basic hero-removed test. It does not pass the stronger approved-signature test: downstream sections do not visibly read as mutations of the same industrial batch instrument.

## Major revisions

1. Make the chronometer/ledger bus perceptibly survive and transform through origin, field notes, brew and subscription, with a repeated material component or unmistakable state lineage visible in still frames as well as motion.
2. Make the selected 3D treatment honestly perceptible through depth, viewpoint, occlusion or another spatial behavior; otherwise reclassify it as layered 2D rather than claiming substantive 3D.
3. Recapture directly inspectable temporal evidence that proves first-viewport response, cross-section handoffs, both interaction peaks, physical input response and the reduced-motion translation.
4. Tune the 320px typography and control scale, and integrate the expanded mobile menu into the ledger system without creating an abrupt oversized interruption.

## Functional and media honesty

The screenshots make conservative, plausible functional claims: product prices, add targets, subscription CTA, contact fields and legal/footer routes are shown without fake success states. Runtime functionality cannot be certified visually. Media appears internally coherent and compositionally integrated, with no obvious broken assets or untreated stock rectangles in the permitted screenshots.
